The Air Max 90 is one of the most popular Nike sneakers ever, and the Air Max 2017 is a very different take on the same Air Max idea. If you are trying to pick between them, or just want to get the size right on a 90, this guide covers the stuff people actually ask.

## What is an Air Max, anyway?

Air Max sneakers use a Max Air unit, which is a pressurized air bag in the sole. The first Air Max, the Air Max 1, came out in 1987 and made that air bag visible through the midsole. The Air Max 90 followed in 1990 and kept the visible bag while adding a bolder look. The Air Max 2017 came much later, and it carries the air through the whole length of the sole instead of just the heel.

## Air Max 90 sizing and fit

The most common advice is that the Air Max 90 fits true to size. If you wear a size 10 in most Nike sneakers, a 10 in the Air Max 90 is the safe bet. A few other things people tend to notice:

- **Width:** it is a medium-width shoe. Many people with wide feet still manage true to size, and going up half a size is a common choice if the front feels tight.
- **Toe box:** reviewers describe it as slightly rounded and about average in room, with no alternate widths offered.

If you are between sizes, think about how you wear them. Thicker socks and wide feet usually point to the bigger size. A snug, secure feel points to the smaller one. You can check the US size on every pair we have on our [Air Max 90 page](/nike-air-max-90/).

## What makes the Air Max 90 feel the way it does

The Air Max 90 has a Max Air unit in the heel and a foam midsole that runs the full length. That means it has a plush heel and a softer-feeling midsole than you would expect from a basic lifestyle shoe. The upper mixes mesh, leather or suede-style overlays and some plastic detailing, depending on the colorway. It is a sneaker you can wear all day, and many people do.

## The Air Max 2017 in a nutshell

The Air Max 2017 uses a full-length Max Air unit under the foot, along with a mesh upper that is molded for a sleeker, more modern look. The big difference you notice is the shape. It looks lower, sleeker and more modern than the 90.

Reviewers who have tested it commonly say it fits true to size too, but they also note that the front of the shoe feels snug. If your feet run wide, that is the spot to watch, and half a size up is the usual workaround. Our pairs are on our [Air Max 2017 page](/nike-air-max-2017/).

## Air Max 90 vs Air Max 2017

| | Air Max 90 | Air Max 2017 |
|---|---|---|
| Air unit | Heel only, with a foam midsole | Full length |
| Look | Classic, layered, a bit chunky | Sleek, modern, low-profile |
| Upper | Mesh with overlays, leather or suede-style panels depending on the colorway | One-piece molded mesh |
| Fit | Usually true to size, medium width | Usually true to size, snug in the front |
| Feel | Plush heel, softer midsole | Firmer, more even feel across the sole |
| Style | Retro, goes with jeans and casual outfits | Athletic, goes with joggers and shorts |

The honest takeaway is that these are two different shoes. The 90 is the retro, layered classic with a soft heel. The 2017 is the sleek, athletic-looking one with the full-length air.

## Which one should you get?

- **Get the Air Max 90** if you want the classic retro look, a softer and plusher feel, and the easiest, most predictable sizing.
- **Get the Air Max 2017** if you like a sleeker, more modern shape and a one-piece upper, and your feet are not wide.

Neither is a pure performance running shoe these days. They are both lifestyle sneakers, so pick on look and fit more than on specs.

## Keeping them clean

Mesh and suede-style panels pick up dirt fast. A soft brush and a damp cloth handle most of it. If you live somewhere hot and rainy like Southwest Florida, give them a day to dry in the shade and not in a hot car. Heat is hard on glue and foam.
